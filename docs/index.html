FNaFb Co-op

Five Nights at F***boy's: Complete Collection with the online co-op mod, on your phone. Co-op, versus and public rooms work with players on PC.

Your game is on this device

Turn your phone sideways. Tap CO-OP / VERSUS on the title screen or the Co-op button in game for online play.

Add your copy of the game

  1. On your PC, find the game's folder (the one with Game.exe and the www folder) and zip it: right click > Compress to ZIP file (Windows) or Compress (Mac).
  2. Put the .zip on your phone: AirDrop, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or a cable.
  3. Tap below and pick it. It's copied onto this phone once; after that the game works offline.
Keep this screen open until it's done.

Good to know

  • iPhone: tap Share > Add to Home Screen and start the game from there. It runs full screen, and iOS keeps your saves (Safari can clear a website's data after a few weeks unused).
  • Your saves stay on this phone. The co-op mod updates itself every time you start the game.
  • Your game is only copied onto this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
